Reasons from the rule

    Ease is fixed at 1. A higher score moves this card earlier in the queue; the number is not a probability.

    The difficult decision

    Recent misses raise priority, while recently shown cards receive a penalty. The rule must balance repeated practice with variety. A transparent score makes that tradeoff inspectable.
